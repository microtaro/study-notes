AI and trust

Can the trust that came from people reading code move to another basis?

Created 2026-11-16

The question

As AI writes more of the code, people can no longer read and check all of it. To trust what gets built anyway, there has to be a basis other than reading.

This theme reads papers that studied real company records and experiments, and looks at what could serve as that basis and where it falls short.

Paper explainers

  • AI Writes Faster Than Humans Can Review

    A paper on the records of a company that set out to double engineering productivity with AI, over more than two years. AI raised PR counts the longer people kept using it, but review fell behind, and AI review replaced part of human review.

    Original paper: arXiv:2607.01904v3. English version added 2026-11-16
